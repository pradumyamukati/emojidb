What is EmojiDB?

EmojiDB is a curated emoji database and copy-and-paste library published at emojidb.info. Instead of scrolling endlessly through a system emoji keyboard, EmojiDB organizes characters by keyword, mood, and common search intent — hearts, cute symbols, laugh reactions, flags, kaomoji, and more — so you can find the right glyph in seconds and paste it into WhatsApp, Instagram, Discord, TikTok, email, or docs.

EmojiDB is designed for both everyday chatters and people who work with text professionally: social managers, creators, support teams, designers, and developers who need reliable Unicode characters without installing apps or creating accounts. Every collection on EmojiDB focuses on practical use — tap once, copy, paste — with related pages that deepen meaning and context rather than dumping an unsorted wall of faces.

EmojiDB FAQ

Is EmojiDB free?

Yes. Core search and copy-paste on EmojiDB at emojidb.info are free and do not require an account.

How is EmojiDB different from my phone’s emoji keyboard?

EmojiDB adds keyword search, themed collections, kaomoji, combo building, and desktop-friendly copy — while the EmojiDB emoji keyboard still covers the top faces you expect from a phone picker.

Can I use EmojiDB emojis on Instagram, WhatsApp, and Discord?

Yes. EmojiDB copies standard Unicode characters that paste into major messaging and social apps, subject to each platform’s font support.
